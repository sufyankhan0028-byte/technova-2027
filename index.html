Everything you need to know before stepping onto the battle arena floor.

TECHNOVA 2027 is open to all enrolled undergraduate and graduate students from any accredited college or university worldwide. Recent graduates (within 1 year of graduation) and high school seniors aged 18+ are also warmly welcome!
